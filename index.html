The Problem

The 72-Hour Window.

When a structure collapses, responders have a 72-hour window to rescue survivors. Today, agencies in Bangladesh and South Asia rely on manual searching — a slow, dangerous process.


Current tactical UGVs (Unmanned Ground Vehicles) are prohibitively expensive ($70K-$200K) and rely on heavy 3D sensors that choke constrained wireless networks in disaster zones. They are built for high-bandwidth environments, not reality.

The Limitations We Face

  • Human responders cannot safely enter unstable structures.
  • Existing robotic solutions are too expensive for developing nations.
  • Dense 3D mapping data (15-21 Mbps) fails over congested ad-hoc networks.
  • Visual cameras fail in zero-visibility, smoke-filled environments.
Our Solution

Computational Offloading & Edge AI.

We engineered an autonomous rover that maps in 3D using only lightweight 2D data, while detecting survivors via thermally-trained AI.

Push-Broom 3D Mapping

Instead of transmitting 3D point clouds, the rover sends lightweight 2D LiDAR slices (85ms delay). A base-station algorithm constructs the full 3D map.

YOLOv11m Thermal AI

Custom-trained on the OpenThermalPose dataset with CLAHE augmentation, achieving 89.2% mAP@50 to detect partially occluded survivors.

Extreme Cost Efficiency

Built with a dual-ESP microcontroller architecture and D-Robotics RDK X5 compute module. Total hardware BOM is under $500 USD.

Interactive 3D Point Cloud (Approach 1 - LiDAR)

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Generated autonomously via our push-broom LiDAR strategy.

Validation

Webots Simulation Framework.

Our hardware and software stack was rigorously tested using Webots and OMNeT++. Below are the validated real-world robotic simulations for our sensor arrays.

Approach 1: LiDAR Only

Push-broom mapping. Optimal network performance (85ms delay) but lacks color context.

Approach 2: RGB-D Camera

High-resolution depth and color, but chokes bandwidth (max 30s delay in mesh network).

Approach 3: RTAB-Map Fusion

Combines LiDAR geometry with RGB textures for the ultimate 3D map.
